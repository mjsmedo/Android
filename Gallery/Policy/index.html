Gallery — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18-Nov-2026

Gallery is a photo and video gallery, video player and media manager for Android. This policy explains what the app can access and what happens to it. The summary is simple: your pictures, videos and data never leave your device.

Data the app accesses

Your pictures and videos (All files access). The app asks for the MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission ("All files access") so it can show every picture and video on your device, in every folder, and let you copy, move, rename and delete them while keeping their original dates. The app reads these files only to show them to you and to carry out the actions you choose. It does not read, collect or analyse any other files.

Fingerprint, face or screen lock (optional). If you choose to unlock the Vault with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, the check is done entirely by Android. The app never receives or stores any biometric data; Android only tells it whether the check succeeded.

Wallpaper. The SET_WALLPAPER permission is used only when you choose "Set as wallpaper" for a picture.

Files from other apps. When another app asks Gallery to open or pick a picture or video, Gallery reads only that file, and only to show it or to return your choice to that app.

Data the app stores

Everything the app keeps is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your settings and preferences, favourites, pinned and excluded folders, and where you stopped watching each video;
  • thumbnails it makes so the gallery scrolls quickly (Settings → Clear thumbnail cache deletes them);
  • the Vault: pictures and videos you move into it, kept inside the app's own storage, and your Vault PIN, which is stored only as a one-way hash, never as the PIN itself.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above, including anything still in the Vault. Restore anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

Data the app shares

None. Gallery does not have internet access: it does not request the Internet permission and contains no networking code, so it cannot send anything off your device. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services, no tracking and no user accounts.

Data leaves the app only when you choose to send it, for example:

  • Share or Open with, which hand the chosen files to an app of your choice through Android;
  • Export settings, which saves a settings file wherever you choose.

Android backup

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's settings (such as preferences, favourites and pinned folders) in your device backup, stored in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. The Vault, its pictures and videos, and its PIN are always excluded from backups and from phone-to-phone transfers. The app itself never uploads anything.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
