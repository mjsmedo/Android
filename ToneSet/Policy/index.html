ToneSet — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

ToneSet ("the app") sets audio files as your ringtone, notification sound or alarm sound on Android. This policy explains what the app can access and what happens to it. The summary is simple: nothing you do in ToneSet leaves your device.

Data the app accesses

One audio file at a time, chosen by you. ToneSet has no permission to browse your storage. When you tap to choose a sound, Android's own file picker opens and hands the app access to only the single file you select. The app reads that file's name and its audio content so it can play a preview and copy it into your sound library.

Your current sound settings. The app reads which sounds are currently set as your ringtone, notification and alarm so it can show them to you, and writes to those settings when you ask it to set a new one. This is the WRITE_SETTINGS permission, which Android requires for any app that changes these sounds. It is used for nothing else.

Data the app stores

Two things, both on your device only:

  • A reference to the last file you picked, so the app can reopen it next launch. Stored in the app's private storage.
  • Copies of the sounds you set, placed in your device's standard Ringtones, Notifications and Alarms folders — because Android requires a sound to live there before it can be used as a ringtone. Your original files are never moved, renamed or altered. The app's "Copied files" screen lets you view and delete these copies at any time.

Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. Copies already placed in your sound folders remain on the device, as they are ordinary files in shared storage — delete them from the app first if you want them gone.

Data the app shares

None. ToneSet does not request the internet permission and contains no networking code. It cannot transmit anything anywhere. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, no tracking and no user accounts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
